# Deepanshi — B.Tech (AI & ML) Personal Portfolio & Professional Resume

A modern, futuristic, and responsive personal portfolio website and ATS-compliant resume system tailored for **Deepanshi**, a first-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning student (in collaboration with IBM) at **JECRC University, Jaipur**.

---

## 🌟 Portfolio Aesthetic & Tech Features

- **Color Palette:** Curated light pink (`#EC4899`, `#FDF2F8`), soft lavender (`#8B5CF6`, `#F5F3FF`), and pearlescent white with subtle glowing gradients.
- **Glassmorphism:** Frosted translucent cards with smooth backdrop filters and delicate rose-lavender borders.
- **Interactive AI Neural Canvas:** Custom HTML5 Canvas particle system simulating AI neural connections with responsive mouse interaction.
- **Cyber-Luxe Dark Mode:** Seamless light/dark theme switcher with saved preferences in `localStorage`.
- **Project Showcase & Modal Dialog:** Interactive cards with category filter tabs and in-depth architecture modal views.
- **Authentic Student Narrative:** Highlights genuine first-year momentum — IBM AI curriculum, coding in Python and C, makerspace robotics, hackathons, and creative passions (dancing & cooking).
- **Interactive Contact Form & Copy Tools:** Client-side validation, instant toast feedback, and one-click copy actions.
- **Zero Framework Overhead:** Pure semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (no bulky frameworks), and modern JavaScript ES6+.

## 🚀 How to Open & Customize

### 1. Launching the Portfolio
Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/deepa/OneDrive/Desktop/portfolio/index.html) in your file explorer to open the website in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

### 2. Customizing Links & Handles
Open [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/deepa/OneDrive/Desktop/portfolio/index.html) in your editor and replace:
- `deepanshi-placeholder` with your real GitHub, LinkedIn, and Instagram usernames.
- `deepanshi.student@example.com` with your real college or personal email address.

### 3. Adding or Updating Projects
In [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/deepa/OneDrive/Desktop/portfolio/index.html) and [`script.js`](file:///c:/Users/deepa/OneDrive/Desktop/portfolio/script.js) (under `projectDetails`), you can edit the project titles, descriptions, bullets, and GitHub repository links whenever you build a new project.

### 4. Viewing or Printing the Resume
- Click the **"📄 Resume"** button in the top navigation bar or footer of the portfolio to jump directly to [`resume.html`](file:///c:/Users/deepa/OneDrive/Desktop/portfolio/resume.html).
- From there, click **"🖨️ Print / Save as PDF"** to generate a single-page PDF.
